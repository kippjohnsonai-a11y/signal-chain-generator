# HX Stomp XL platform limits

- One DSP path in practice: **8 blocks maximum** per preset (input/output not counted). The looper, an FX Loop and a volume pedal each use one of those 8.
- DSP budget is the real constraint: one DSP for the whole preset, and the pedal refuses a block that doesn't fit (on a real push, Red Squeeze + Poly Downtune + Pitch Wham + phaser + amp + cab + spring reverb + looper overflowed and the amp was refused). Rough costs: amp ~30%, cab ~8%, polyphonic pitch (Poly Capo/Downtune/Pitch/Wham/Detune) ~40%, premium reverbs (Glitz, Ganymede, Searchlights, Plateaux, Dynamic*) ~18%, other reverbs ~10%, delays ~8%, modulation ~6%, other pitch ~12%, looper ~8%, drives ~4%, dynamics ~3%, EQ ~2%. Keep the total under ~100%. For a song in a lower tuning, have the player retune the guitar (say so in the tips) rather than spending a third of the DSP on a Poly Downtune, unless the chain is otherwise light.
- In this app, amps carry their own cab (Amp+Cab), so a separate cab block is only needed when a specific cab is requested and the budget allows it.
- **4 snapshots** per preset, stepped through in order with SNAP up/down, so put them in the order the song is played. Snapshots recall block on/off states and up to 64 parameter values; they do not change models or block order.
- Snapshot names show at most 10 characters; preset names at most 16.
- Footswitches: top row FS1 FS2 FS3 MODE, bottom row FS4 FS5 FS6 TAP. The player uses Stomp Mode "4 Switch" with Up/Down Switches set to "Snapshots": FS1/FS4 step up/down through the 4 snapshots, and FS2, FS3, FS5, FS6 are stomps. The app assigns those itself: looper on FS6, then by priority wah, drive, delay, volume boost, modulation (FS5, FS2, FS3). So at most 3-4 blocks get a stomp; everything else must be handled by the snapshots.
- The app adds a 'Gain' volume-boost block (+4 dB, off by default, on its own switch) whenever a block slot is free. Do not choose a 'Gain' block yourself, and leave one of the 8 slots free when the tone allows it.
- Expression: two external expression inputs (EXP 1, EXP 2). An external pedal without a toe switch cannot engage a wah by itself - the wah block goes on a footswitch and its Pedal parameter on EXP 1.
- The built-in looper is the "One Switch Looper" block (press: record, play, overdub; double-press: stop; hold: undo, or clear when stopped). Snapshots never change its on/off state. Put it last in the chain so loops keep the tone they were recorded with even when snapshots change.
