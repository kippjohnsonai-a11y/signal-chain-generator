# Interpreting player feedback

Translate subjective feedback into concrete moves. Make the smallest set of changes that addresses it, and say what you changed.

- "Muddy", "boomy", "woolly", "undefined": raise low cut (cab or post-amp EQ) to 90-140 Hz; reduce amp Bass by 0.5-1.5; reduce Drive a little (gain adds mud); tighten with a TS-style boost (low gain, high level) in front for high-gain; reduce reverb Mix or raise the reverb's low cut; for humbuckers especially.
- "Fizzy", "harsh", "ice-pick", "buzzy", "brittle": lower high cut to 5.5-7 kHz; reduce Presence/Treble; reduce Drive; for single coils lower Treble further.
- "Thin", "weak", "no body": lower low cut to 70-80 Hz; raise Bass and Mid; more Drive or a drive pedal level; move toward a bigger-cab model if allowed.
- "Dull", "dark", "muffled", "flat": raise Treble/Presence; lower cut filters opened up (higher high cut); raise amp Bright if present.
- "Too much gain", "too fuzzy", "too saturated": reduce amp Drive and drive-pedal Gain; raise Channel Volume to keep level.
- "Not enough gain", "not heavy enough", "no sustain": raise Drive, or engage/raise a drive; for single coils this is often the fix.
- "Scooped", "gets lost in the mix", "no cut": raise Mid (and 750 Hz-1.5 kHz on an EQ); reduce Bass/reverb.
- "Too compressed", "squashed", "lifeless": lower compressor amount / Drive; raise Master on non-MV amps for more dynamics.
- "Noisy", "hiss", "hum": add or tighten a gate (raise Threshold), lower Drive; note single-coil hum.
- "Washy", "too much reverb/delay", "blurry": lower Mix, shorten Decay/Feedback, raise their low cut.
- "Too dry", "sterile", "boxy": add a little room/plate reverb (Mix 10-15%), open up high cut.
- "Too quiet"/"too loud" (whole preset or a snapshot): adjust amp Channel Volume or the relevant block Level.
- "Solo doesn't jump out": raise level in the solo snapshot by ~2-3 dB, add a boost and delay, increase mids.
- Feedback about a specific section ("the chorus is too...") changes that snapshot only.
- If feedback suggests a different model ("should be more like a Marshall"), swap the block.
